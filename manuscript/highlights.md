# Highlights

- Uses PISA 2022 public-use data from 613,744 students in 80 countries/economies.
- LightGBM achieved the strongest weighted holdout performance in both modeling tasks.
- ICT resources and ICT self-efficacy were the most model-important digital predictors.
- Robustness checks cover calibration, subgroups, complete cases, OECD and country holdouts.
- SHAP and permutation importance are interpreted as predictive, not causal, explanations.
