# Student Task Checklist

Codex can handle data processing, code, analysis, writing, tables, and submission documents once the source files are available. Your tasks should stay limited to items that require account access, school policy knowledge, or academic judgment.

## Required From You

- Download PISA 2022 student questionnaire data and place it in `data/raw/`.
- Download PISA 2022 school questionnaire data and place it in `data/raw/` if available.
- Download or save the PISA 2022 codebook and technical report in `data/raw/` or `docs/`.
- Confirm author list and author order with your supervisor.
- Confirm whether your school requires an ethics or public-data exemption statement.
- Confirm whether the first submitted version should use a global sample, OECD-only sample, China/East Asia subset, or another scope.

## Optional But Useful

- Ask your supervisor whether the paper should emphasize education technology, educational measurement, or applied statistics.
- Create a Zotero collection for the project and export BibTeX later.
- Provide any university journal ranking requirements.

## Do Not Spend Time On

- Manual table formatting.
- Rewriting Python code.
- Manually copying model outputs into the manuscript.
- Manually producing SHAP plots.
- Formatting references by hand.
